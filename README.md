## Pulmonology Medical Billing Services Designed for Respiratory Care Excellence

The complexity of pulmonology billing extends well beyond standard office visit claims. Pulmonologists manage a broad spectrum of conditions — from asthma and emphysema to pulmonary fibrosis, pleural disease, and lung cancer — each requiring highly specific CPT codes, ICD-10 diagnoses, and payer-defined documentation standards. Reimbursement for pulmonary function tests (PFTs), bronchoscopies, thoracentesis, chest tube placements, and ventilator management must be coded with precision to avoid delays, downcoding, or denials.

Virtual and in-office pulmonology billing requires an in-depth understanding of procedure-specific modifier usage, payer policy distinctions, and documentation requirements. We ensure that every encounter is coded correctly — reducing the risk of denials or post-payment audits. As payer guidelines shift and value-based care models expand into respiratory medicine, our billing framework adapts to maintain compliance while protecting reimbursement integrity.

Beyond claim submission, our pulmonology billing strategy includes eligibility verification, benefits coordination, and payer communication tailored specifically to respiratory health services. We work closely with practices offering diagnostic consultations, pulmonary rehabilitation, remote monitoring, and in-patient respiratory therapy to streamline the entire billing lifecycle. This proactive oversight reduces aging receivables and enhances predictable revenue streams.

## Respiratory Therapy Billing with Accuracy and Long-Term Compliance

Respiratory therapy billing occupies a unique and often underappreciated position within the healthcare revenue cycle. Therapists and pulmonary care teams deliver critical services — including incentive spirometry, aerosol medication therapy, chest physiotherapy, mechanical ventilation management, and continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) monitoring — that require precise documentation and payer-specific claim formats to receive appropriate reimbursement.

Undercoding or missed charges in respiratory therapy billing can silently erode revenue over time. Patients with chronic conditions such as COPD, asthma, cystic fibrosis, and pulmonary hypertension often require recurring services across extended care episodes. Each touchpoint in the care continuum represents a billable encounter that must be captured, coded, and submitted accurately. Our billing specialists understand the nuances of bundled versus separately billable respiratory services and apply consistent coding logic to maximize legitimate reimbursement while minimizing audit risk.

In addition, respiratory therapy billing requires clear coordination with inpatient and outpatient facilities, home health agencies, and durable medical equipment (DME) suppliers. Co-management claims, supervision billing, and split-shared visit guidelines add another layer of complexity. Our team manages these crossover scenarios with expertise, ensuring that claims are compliant, complete, and submitted on time — protecting both financial performance and the professional integrity of your therapy team.

Our billing services ensure that each therapy session, ventilator check, breathing treatment, and patient education encounter is coded correctly and supported by appropriate documentation. ## Sleep Medicine Billing That Supports Diagnostic Precision and Care Continuity

Sleep medicine practices operate within a distinct billing framework that includes both facility-based and home-based diagnostic services. From in-lab polysomnography (PSG) and multiple sleep latency tests (MSLT) to home sleep apnea tests (HSAT) and CPAP/BiPAP titration studies, each procedure carries specific CPT codes, technical and professional component distinctions, and medical necessity requirements that must be clearly documented in order to receive timely reimbursement.

Proper documentation of clinical indications for sleep studies is central to sleep medicine billing compliance. Our team reviews medical records to ensure alignment with payer guidelines and reduce audit risks. Accurate capture of interpretation services, follow-up management, and durable medical equipment (DME) authorization ensures that the full scope of care is represented in every claim. By proactively managing coverage limitations, prior authorization requirements, and visit caps, we help sleep medicine providers reduce denied claims and accelerate the payment cycle.

Our billing process also addresses the growing role of home sleep testing and remote care management in sleep medicine. As payers increasingly shift approval toward lower-cost home-based diagnostics, our team stays current with evolving policy requirements to ensure that HSAT claims meet technical standards and documentation thresholds. The result is a streamlined billing system that accurately reflects the diagnostic and therapeutic value delivered in each patient encounter — protecting revenue across both the technical and professional sides of care.
